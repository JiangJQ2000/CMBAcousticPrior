CMB compression & Gaussian distance priors

Late-time agnostic CMB Prior

The acoustic features of the CMB constrain the angular sound horizon and the physical baryon and cold-dark-matter densities. This prior retains those three correlated quantities while removing or marginalizing the late-time contributions to the observed spectra.
